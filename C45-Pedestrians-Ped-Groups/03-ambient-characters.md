# C45.03 — Ambient Named Characters

> Ambient Named Characters — grounded in the verified level-init vocabulary.

`AddAmbientCharacter("apu","m0_apu_place",1.8)` places a specific, recognizable NPC at a named locator with a spawn radius — distinct from the anonymous crowd. Many are bound to interiors (Apu in the Kwik-E-Mart, Lisa at the school, C41). Why: story/flavor characters need to be *specific* and reliably present, unlike the random crowd. Bend it: add/replace named NPCs, move their locator, change the radius.
