# C47.6 — Modding Placement & Behaviour

> Modding Placement & Behaviour — grounded in the verified level-script vocabulary.

Edit the level script: re-point AddSpawnPointByLocatorScript at different locators or change ranges to move encounters; add AddAmbientNPCWaypoint routes; attach AddBehaviour jobs. New locators require adding them to the world art (C8). New actor models must be loaded (C39.4). Native: hook the behaviour/AI classes (C42/C28.5) for custom behaviour. Reversible, single-player (C28.6).
